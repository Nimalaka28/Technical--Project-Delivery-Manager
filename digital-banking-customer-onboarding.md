# 📱 Digital Banking & Customer Onboarding — BFSI Technical Project Delivery

## 📌 Project Overview

A sample BFSI digital transformation project demonstrating the end-to-end delivery of a **Digital Banking and Customer Onboarding platform** for mobile and web banking channels.

The objective is to provide customers with a secure, simple and digitally enabled onboarding journey while integrating customer onboarding services with banking, KYC, authentication and account-opening systems.

## 🔄 Digital Onboarding Journey

**Customer Registration → Mobile Verification → Customer Data → KYC → Identity Verification → Risk Checks → Account Selection → Consent → Account Opening → Confirmation**

### Typical Customer Journey

1. Customer starts onboarding through mobile or web channel
2. Mobile number / email is verified
3. Customer provides required personal information
4. KYC information is captured and validated
5. Identity verification is performed
6. Required documents are uploaded
7. Eligibility and risk checks are completed
8. Customer selects the appropriate account/product
9. Digital consent is captured
10. Account-opening request is submitted
11. Core banking system creates the customer/account
12. Customer receives confirmation and account details
